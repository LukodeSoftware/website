Frequently Asked Questions

An Accessibility Audit evaluates your web application against the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) to identify barriers affecting users with disabilities and ensure legal compliance.

We test against WCAG 2.1 and 2.2 standards at Level A and Level AA, ensuring compliance with international regulations including the European Accessibility Act (EAA) and ADA.

Since June 2025, non-compliance with the EAA carries severe financial and operational sanctions across EU member states. Statutory fines reach up to €100,000 per violation in Germany, €250,000 in France, and up to €1,000,000 (or up to 10% of annual turnover) in Spain and the Netherlands. Regulators can also issue court injunctions to halt digital sales, block checkout flows, or levy daily recurring coercive penalties until barriers are fixed. For a comprehensive overview by country, read our guide on European Accessibility Act Penalties: What Non-Compliance Costs Businesses.
