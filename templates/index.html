About Us

Lukode is an independent, expert-led software quality engineering company specializing in testing modern applications, complex APIs, and AI-powered systems.

The owner is an experienced AI/ML QA Engineer working on Google's projects, bringing specialized expertise in testing AI/ML features, large language models, API workflows, and digital accessibility.

We partner with product teams and engineering leaders to provide unbiased, end-to-end quality engineering across the release lifecycle—helping teams catch critical failure modes, eliminate model hallucinations, and deploy stable, compliant software.

Our Why

Software quality cannot be an afterthought, especially when integrating probabilistic AI components or mission-critical workflows. Flaws in model reasoning, untested edge cases, and accessibility gaps directly affect user trust and operational stability.

We believe in systematic, independent verification. By combining disciplined testing methodologies with automated evaluation suites, we help teams ship dependable software that functions as intended under real-world conditions.

Our quality principles:
  • Validate AI systems for consistency, safety, and factual accuracy.
  • Identify functional, regression, and integration defects prior to release.
  • Ensure digital accessibility across platforms following WCAG standards.
  • Provide transparent, reproducible test documentation and defect analysis.
  • Help product teams maintain high release velocity without compromising reliability.
